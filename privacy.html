What we collect and why

When you join the waitlist we collect your email address and, if you choose to give it, your country.

We use them only to tell you when Gradays launches. If we'd like to send you anything else, such as a beta invite or a survey, we'll ask you separately first.

How it's handled

  • The sign-up form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers it to our Gmail inbox, provided by Google.
  • This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the fonts load.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses.
  • We don't use cookies or tracking scripts.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until Gradays launches plus a reasonable period after, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your email at any time. Just email pakplome78@gmail.com.

Children

The waitlist isn't meant for children without a parent's permission. If you're under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before joining.

Changes to this policy
